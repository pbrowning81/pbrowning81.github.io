About

I was born and raised in Charlottesville, Virginia, and started at Capital One at eighteen. I spent close to two decades there, growing from an entry-level job to Director and picking up a real passion for technology and technical execution along the way. I've kept investing in it since, through graduate work, certifications, and lately teaching myself to build.

I moved to Delaware about ten years ago, and today I'm a Vice President of Product Management at JPMorgan Chase, in reference data. The work I care most about now is how product management changes when AI is genuinely within reach: the foundations a large organization needs to use it, and the operating model that lets a team build instead of just writing specs. It's the part that doesn't depend on me being in the room.

Outside work, I've got four grown kids and a standing preference for being near the water. I fish, I'm outdoors whenever I can be, and I follow sports closely. Most of my best thinking happens away from a screen.
